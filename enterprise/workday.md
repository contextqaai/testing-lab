# Workday enterprise lab

**Route:** Personal development tenant beta or existing test tenant

## What is established

Workday currently advertises open developer registration and requests for personal development tenants in beta. Verify allocation and demo rights before relying on it.

A newer public developer route exists; older company-admin-only documentation does not capture every current option. Development-tenant access is not proof that all native HCM/payroll modules or role flows are available.

No account or tenant was created. Do not use employee records or imply full Workday product coverage from an Extend exercise.
